Wield ultimate cosmic power to explode, freeze, and swallow entire worlds in Annihilate Earth, a satisfying, physics-based sandbox simulator where space is your playground and destruction is your art form. Experiment with a unique arsenal of weapons, travel across diverse planets within the solar system, and watch celestial bodies collapse in real-time. With intuitive point-and-shoot mechanics, it is easy to pick up but offers infinite ways to tear down planets.
How to Play Annihilate Earth
Your goal in Annihilate Earth is simple: absolute planetary destruction. Choose a target planet and lay waste to it using a suite of terrifying weapons. Pierce deep through a planet's crust using high-energy Lasers, blast massive craters with Asteroids, or summon Cthulhu itself to tear apart the land. You can even release orbital Black Holes that pull planets apart with gravity, launch devouring space Worms, or try your luck by dropping a few mystery crates.
Discover and Unlock Weapons
Start your journey with basic planetary threats like standard Missiles, Lasers, and Asteroids. The more devastation you cause and levels you complete, the more chances you get to spin the Wheel of Weapons to expand your arsenal! Unlocking weapons via victory spinners or in-game shooting stars will grant you access to legendary instruments of doom.
Weapons Categories in Annihilate Earth:
Projectiles & Bombs: Missiles and thermonuclear Nukes for rapid-fire blast damage.
Cosmic Collisions: Asteroids, Shooting Stars, Comets and Moons to slam into the surface.
Monsters & Summons: Devouring space worms, giant fists, Cthulhu summons, and explosive ducks.
Singularities: Black holes that warp terrain and swallow matter.
Unusual Weapons: Giant swords, giant fists, and giant bowling balls that get lodged into the crust.
