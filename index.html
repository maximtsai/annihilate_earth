Annihilate Earth - Free Online Planet Destruction Simulator

Welcome to Annihilate Earth, the ultimate sandbox game designed for planetary destruction enthusiasts. Unleash catastrophic cosmic forces and experience an interactive simulator where you control the end of the world. Take aim at a realistic, rotating Earth and deploy a massive arsenal of weapons to completely fracture, vaporize, and erase civilizations from the cosmos.

Unleash 11 Devastating Planetary Weapons

From tactical strikes to systemic cosmic anomalies, test the limits of gravity and structural integrity with our unblocked weapon wheel:

  • Kinetic & Explosive: Fire rapid-velocity Missiles or drop catastrophic Nuclear Bombs to crack tectonic plates.
  • Cosmic Phenomena: Steer massive Asteroid impacts, ignite terminal Gamma Ray Bursts, or drop high-density Black Holes directly into the core to watch the planet swallow itself.
  • Mythical & Extraterrestrial Forces: Summon the power of legendary blades like Excalibur, coordinate direct lunar collisions with the Moon, unleash the ancient space terror Cthulhu, launch world-eating space Worms, or shatter continents with a massive Cosmic Fist strike.

Explore and Demolish the Solar System

Don't stop at Earth. Use our celestial navigation deck to alter targets on the fly. Test weapon mechanics on the rocky surface of Mars, penetrate the thick atmospheres of ice and gas giants like Neptune and Jupiter, or go completely terminal by attempting to trigger a supernova on the Sun itself.

Dynamic Physics-Based Destruction

Every crater, tear, and fracture recalculates the remaining planet mass in real-time. Watch remaining planetary crust adjust to localized gravitational pulls as you shave layers down to zero. Annihilate Earth requires no installs or downloads—play completely free in any modern mobile or desktop web browser.
